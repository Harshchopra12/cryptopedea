# History of Money

Welcome to CryptoPedea!

Money has evolved over thousands of years. Before modern currencies existed, people exchanged goods directly through a system known as **barter**.

## What is Barter?

Barter is the direct exchange of goods and services without using money.

Example:

- Rice for milk
- Wheat for clothes
- Gold for livestock

## Problems with Barter

- Difficult to find someone who wanted exactly what you had.
- No standard value for goods.
- Hard to store wealth.

These challenges eventually led to the invention of money.

---

### Key Takeaway

> Money evolved because barter was inefficient.
